WHAT AVA DOES FOR YOUR EVENT

AVA is the intelligence running through Event Pal. Here's what she handles.

📝Reads your event briefTurns one form into the venue plan: gates, bars, tills, screens and sponsor slots, and lists anything still missing.
✨Builds your graphics packFrom your logo, colours and flyer: openers, lower thirds, custom effects and transitions made for your event.
👀Walks the event every 2 minutesGates, tills, kitchens, screens, boxes, sites and phones, all checked on a schedule and the moment something changes.
🔎Works out whyBefore she alerts, she investigates. One till down while the next is fine means that tablet, not the wifi. She says how sure she is.
📨Tells the right personTelegram alerts to you, your manager or your screen operator, and notes on the right till. Urgent ones repeat until someone taps Got it.
📈Learns from your teamReply to an alert with what it really was, and she checks that first next time.
💬Answers in plain wordsHow many are in? Which bar is busiest? Is the sponsor rotation on time? Ask on Telegram or Pal Monitor.
✅Acts on your say-soPlay a sponsor now, send stock to bar 2, raise a limit: each instruction read back and logged before it runs, by role.
🎯Keeps sponsors wholeCounts every slot, works missed ones back in with your approval, and keeps proof of every play.
📊Writes your reportTickets, crowd by the hour, takings by bar, inclusive servings, stock and sponsor proof, ready next morning.

THE EVENT PROTOCOL

Every Event Pal event runs to the same seven stages, so nothing is left to the night.

BriefYou fill in the event brief: tickets and access, food and drink, screens and technical needs, sponsors. AVA flags the gaps.
BuildVenue plan, ticket types, gates, bars and tills, stock issue, screens, graphics pack and sponsor rotation set up and checked.
RehearseA full run before the day: every gate phone scans, every till sells, every screen shows, every scene and cue played through.
DoorsAVA's walk-around before doors open. Gates go live, the crowd count starts, the first stock is confirmed at every bar.
ShowLive watch through the night: alerts, announcements, sponsor rotation, crowd moments and top-ups, on Pal Monitor and Telegram.
CloseBars count blind, cash is counted, stock is returned, and the numbers lock.
ReportNext morning: the full report, as a spreadsheet, with sponsor proof of play.
